    How this advisor works

    This is deterministic analysis, not generative AI. “AI Advisor” is the navigation name. Figures are based on recorded transactions, budgets, goals, account balances, loan records and commitments in the selected environment and currency. Currency selection filters records; it does not convert money.

    Month reports compare equivalent dates. Current month values are month-to-date. Account balances, upcoming payments and goal balances are current as of the server date, separately labeled. A recorded month with no entries does not prove zero activity. Refresh after changing your records.

    Scenarios are independent illustrations: savings reductions, land funding and business capital are not added together or reserved automatically. Planning inputs can be saved in this browser, separately by user, environment and currency. No financial records are changed.

    Investment education: SEBI — factors to consider before investing · SEBI — managing investment risk. Planning rules are not live product recommendations, tax advice or guaranteed returns.
